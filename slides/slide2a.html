one shuffle (seed 1). A good predictor would sit on the dashed line.

The guess is bad at values, mediocre at order — and that is still enough, because we only ever ask it for a shortlist of 5.

knnr trained on 25 labels: 10 MOOT files × 50 shuffles, 50:50 train:test
dataerr %ρtop5 %

err % = 100·MAE / MAE(always predict the mean of the 25 labels). 100 = no better than a constant.
ρ = Spearman rank correlation, predicted vs actual, over every test row.
top5 % = rank of the truly best row among the 5 best-predicted (what slide 2 reports).

Two files beat the constant by nothing at all (pom3d 100, Wine_quality 105), and ρ falls to 0.11 — yet their top-5 still beats the 16.3% that checking 5 random rows gets.
