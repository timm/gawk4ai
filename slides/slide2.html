1. Shuffle, then bin: the whole protocol is one blank line

shuf shuffles the rows and prints ONE blank line inside, with the training rows before it and the held-out last 25% after. That line is the only protocol: bins takes mu and sd from the rows before it, so nothing the held-out rows contain can reach the stats that bin them. No tag column, no counter, no second pass.

shuf.awk — shuffle, split, mark the seam


    

    

      
$ shuf.awk Seed=1 diabetes.csv
